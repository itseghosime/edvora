Clarity & Assurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from school proprietors, bursars, and enrolled families.

Guardians receive a single linked overview of all registered siblings under their verified phone number. While statements can be settled child-by-child or in bulk, each payment automatically allocates to that individual child’s audited ledger tranche.
All card transactions and virtual dedicated account transfers route directly through Paystack. Even if your phone disconnects mid-payment, our server receives the asynchronous webhook notification and updates the school register immediately with no lost funds.
Edvora deliberately implements one-way administrative notifications to preserve educator well-being and instructional focus. Parents receive comprehensive term progress updates and can book appointments through the school office telephone desk.
Class teachers have a secure drafting view to record CA tests (continuous assessments) and final exam grades. Once a class ledger is finished, it is locked and submitted to the Head Teacher for institutional review before any PDF report card is compiled and made accessible.
