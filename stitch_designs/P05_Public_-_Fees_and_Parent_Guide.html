Helpful Clarity

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Clear answers regarding payment options, sibling adjustments, and fee certainty.

tune Are there unexpected mid-term levies?

No. Our published schedule encompasses instructional apparatus, labs, and arts. We do not issue unannounced weekend levies or ad-hoc demands for basic curriculum tools.

diversity_3 Is there a sibling fee accommodation?

Yes. Families with three or more concurrently enrolled pupils receive a 5% tuition adjustment on the third child’s tuition, applied directly in the bursary portal upon enrolment.

history_edu What if bank transfers delay confirmation?

If a payment is still pending, keep its reference and contact the school office. Your balance updates after verification.
