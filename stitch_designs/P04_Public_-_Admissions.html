Required Documents Checklist

To assist our administrative registrar in drafting your pupil's permanent student file, please prepare clear photocopies of the following items prior to Stage 4.

badge
Pupil's Birth Certificate or Age Declaration

Government-issued NPC statutory certificate verifying date of birth for correct early-years cohort placement.

portrait
Recent Passport Photographs (2 Copies)

Clear frontal portraits with white or pale background for school identity badges and safety collection registries.

history_edu
Previous Academic Progress Report

Required for transfers entering Nursery 2 through Primary 6. Helps our educators preserve continuity in phonics and numeracy.

vaccines
Child Immunization Record & Medical Form

Essential for nursery and creche care. Enables our school nurse to record allergies, dietary caveats, and emergency protocols.

home_pin
Guardian Identification & Proof of Address

NIN slip, International Passport, or Driver’s License paired with a recent utility statement in Edo State.

Need help compiling files?

The admissions office can explain which documents are needed and how to submit them.
